This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information the App Itself Collects

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information on its own. There is no account, sign-in, contact, or payment data. Your exercise choices and settings remain on your device only.

Advertising (Third-Party Data)

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). As part of delivering and measuring ads, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Apple advertising identifier, used to deliver, target, and measure advertisements;
  • Advertising data and product-interaction data about your use of the App, used by the ad network to evaluate ad performance.

Before the App can use the advertising identifier for tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies, it asks your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You may decline, and you can restrict advertising personalization at any time in your device’s Settings. Where ads are disabled in the App, no ad-related identifiers are collected.

Third-Party Privacy

The collection and use of data by AdMob is governed by Google’s own privacy policy and ad-terms, which you can review at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where applicable, the App’s ad settings comply with applicable child-privacy requirements.
