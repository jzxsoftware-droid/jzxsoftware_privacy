How to reach us

For questions, bug reports, or feature ideas, email markj.jzx@outlook.com. We typically reply within a couple of days.

Quick troubleshooting

  • No ads appearing? Ads load on-demand and vary by region. If ads are missing, check that you’re online and try again later.
  • Workout doesn’t start? Make sure at least one exercise is set for every suit before dealing.
  • Wrong reps? Card rank sets the rep count (Ace = 1, face cards = 10). You choose which exercise each suit maps to.

Reset local data

DEAL keeps your exercise settings only on your device. To start fresh, delete and reinstall the app, or review settings in the Setup screen.
